## Existing rule — verbatim

- **MOD-136** · Unreviewed · P1 — Change handling: unchanged inputs produce zero edits; repeated runs are idempotent; full rebuild is recovery only. Stage edits, validate, report conflicts, then write only changed files while keeping unrelated notes and human content. Imported deletions get a tombstone/review and never silently erase manually edited notes or unmodeled attachments. Keep an undo manifest of pre-change hashes. Updates are staged for diff/PR approval and never overwrite another contributor's work. (REG-285, V2-26 (Functional Parity))

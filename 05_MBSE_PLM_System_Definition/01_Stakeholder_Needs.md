# Stakeholder needs — organization-wide MBSE and PLM

**Status:** Candidate stakeholder needs. Not requirements or allocated native model notes. Reuse existing `10_Stakeholder_Needs` notes after semantic comparison; avoid duplicates.

| ID | Stakeholder | Need statement | Why / expected value |
|---|---|---|---|
| NEED-P01 | Engineering and product teams | The organization needs one understandable, connected view of product intent, definition and lifecycle records across disciplines. | Avoid contradictory definitions and disconnected handoffs |
| NEED-P02 | Systems engineers and product owners | Engineers need to connect customer and regulatory intent to architecture, behavior, verification and released product configurations. | End-to-end evidence of intended capability |
| NEED-P03 | All engineering disciplines | Engineers need to find and reuse trusted parts, requirements, models, designs and decisions without learning every source tool. | Reduce duplication and onboarding cost |
| NEED-P04 | Product and program management | Decision-makers need to assess effects of proposed changes on product families, configurations, timing, cost, safety and customers before approval. | Prevent avoidable rework and release errors |
| NEED-P05 | Configuration managers and manufacturing | Teams need an unambiguous approved product definition for each variant, revision and manufacturing/release baseline. | Build the correct product with controlled documentation |
| NEED-P06 | Manufacturing and supply chain | Teams need coherent engineering, manufacturing, supplier and service product structures with explicit differences and approved substitutions. | Prevent BOM and sourcing discrepancies |
| NEED-P07 | Quality, regulatory and verification | Reviewers need objective evidence that requirements and risks are addressed by approved designs, tests and dispositions. | Auditability and compliance |
| NEED-P08 | Engineering contributors and reviewers | Contributors need controlled collaborative edits with provenance, approval, conflict management and recoverable history. | Preserve trust and accountability |
| NEED-P09 | Field service and product support | Service teams need to identify an installed product's actual configuration, applicable documents, issues and approved remedies. | Faster and safer support |
| NEED-P10 | Executives and program owners | Leaders need credible coverage, readiness, change, risk and lifecycle dashboards based on auditable data. | Better portfolio decisions |
| NEED-P11 | IT/security and external collaborators | Authorized users need controlled access, identity, retention, information classification and safe interchange across internal/external systems. | Protect intellectual property and regulated records |
| NEED-P12 | Tool owners and integrators | The organization needs an adaptable, standards-based platform that can evolve without breaking existing model identities, source provenance or integrations. | Long-term interoperability and maintainability |
| NEED-P13 | Engineering and manufacturing | Teams need to relate product failure modes, field findings, improvements and corrective actions to affected requirements, designs and releases. | Close the feedback loop |
| NEED-P14 | Customer-facing teams | Teams need accurate, configurable product documentation and controlled customer-facing outputs derived from approved engineering information. | Avoid conflicting external specifications |

### Existing likely overlaps — review, not duplicate
`Engineering Knowledge Base`, `Preserve Imported Knowledge`, `Protect Engineer Changes`, `Accessible Engineering Knowledge`, `Understand Model Impacts`, `Requirements Management`, `Architecture Definition`, and the owner-proposed `Adaptable Tool`. Existing needs are not automatically superseded.

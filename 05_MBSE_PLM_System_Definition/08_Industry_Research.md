Requirements and architecture in this folder are MDSE proposals informed by these sources, not product facts.

| Source | Observed offering | Relevance to MDSE |
|---|---|---|

## Market-derived value patterns (inferences, not measured results)
- More reliable changes: one linkable chain from customer intent through model, release and observed verification.
- Fewer wrong-build errors: released configuration and BOM effectivity available to manufacturing/supply chain.
- Less repeated modeling: reusable definitions, structured identities and multi-discipline navigation.
- Faster reviews: impact analysis, approval history and baseline-specific reports.
- Broader adoption: normal engineering language, search and document viewpoints alongside native SysML.

## Research gaps and validation
Interview roles across manufacturing, supply chain, service, quality and management; record real workflows and pain points. Independently test any interoperability claims before committing to integrations. Compare deployment, licensing, configuration management, permissions and data ownership of candidate products. Gather a representative product change and trace it end-to-end using actual business records.
